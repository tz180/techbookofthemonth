About

Hi! Welcome to Tech Book of The Month.

Tech Book of the Month is a club for anyone interested in technology, building technology companies, or the history of technology. Each month we will read a new technology focused book and uncover different technology themes, business themes, and interesting facts about tech companies from around the world.

The club allows nominations for new books and nominations are voted on anonymously by members. Feel free to nominate a book by emailing tyler@techbookofthemonth.com with your recommendation.
