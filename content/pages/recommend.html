Recommend a Book

Have recommendation for a future book? Submit it here and it may be chosen as the next book we read. Recommendations are anonymous and help curate the best content for our readers.

If you are emphatic about your recommendation, add a few additional notes about why you think the book would be great for the club.

Thanks!

(The original page carried an anonymous submission form; recommendations can also be emailed to tyler@techbookofthemonth.com.)
